## Summary
S2 verdict — Stop hook 'continue: true' is a NO-OP for forced continuation; 'decision: block' + reason IS the working mechanism. Empirically observed in CC 2.1.205 on 2026-07-10 (TASK-57 spike). continue:true was accepted (hook_success) but the session ended normally (num_turns=1, no continuation) — continue only has effect as continue:false. decision:block injected its reason as a user-role 'Stop hook feedback:' message and produced exactly one continuation obeying it (CLI result was the sentinel NAV-S2B-bbebfac5, num_turns=2). Runaway protection verified on both layers: the re-fire after a block carries stop_hook_active=true (harness belt), and the single-shot flag-file fuse consumed correctly with a run-twice subtest showing zero further continuations (mem-037 discipline). v7 consequence: stop_completion builds on decision:block; continue:true ships OFF permanently.
